# Getting started

Prism Survey turns a published measure into a correctly scored scale for a Word questionnaire. Everything stays on this computer: scales are `.pscale` files in your library folder, and questionnaires are `.pstudy` files.

The window has four tabs:

- **Scales**: make, import, check and save one scale at a time. The library of your saved scales is on the left.
- **Questionnaire**: put scales, a cover letter, transitions and demographic questions in order, in sections, and export the whole questionnaire to Word.
- **Comm Directory**: search the Directory of Communication Related Mental Measures and bring a measure in.
- **Help**: these pages, and Check for Updates.

## The usual path

1. Bring a scale in: start from one of the three blank formats, [import a stored scale](topic:import), or [find it in a journal article](topic:article).
2. Confirm and correct every field on [the scale form](topic:scale-form). The [self-check](topic:self-check) points out likely scoring errors as you go.
3. Save it to your [library](topic:library).
4. Build a questionnaire on the [Questionnaire tab](topic:questionnaire), with a [cover letter](topic:cover-letters) and [demographic questions](topic:demographics).
5. [Export it to Word](topic:export) as a respondent copy and a researcher copy.

To see how the pieces fit before you start, open the Questionnaire tab and choose **Open the sample questionnaire**. It is built from made-up practice scales that come with Prism Survey.
