# The scale form

Every field of a scale is on one form, to confirm and correct before anything is saved. A field the importer was unsure of is marked **Check**; press F8 to move to the next one.

## Scale

- **Title** and **Short name**. Tick the box under the tags to keep an item count in the title (as in "Homophobia-16 Scale") in step with the items.
- **Tags**: your own words, to find the scale again. Type a tag and press Enter; tags already in the library are offered as you type.
- **Format**: Likert blank-line (a blank to write a number in), Likert box grid (one box to mark per row) or semantic differential (pairs of opposite adjectives).
- **Number of items**: type how many items the measure has and press Enter. Empty rows are added at the end, or rows are taken from the end; rows with text are only taken after you agree.
- **Points**, **Anchor set** and **Labels on**: the response scale. The table under them sets each point's label, the number it scores and, for a box grid, its box header.
- **Fill the labels from a common set**: 34 five-point label sets (agreement, frequency, likelihood, importance, satisfaction and more). Choosing one fills the labels; edit them as needed. At another point count the first and last label become the ends.

## Instructions

House instructions are rewritten whenever the format, points or anchors change. Instructions kept word for word from a source, or edited by hand, are never rewritten; a change of format is flagged instead. **Use house instructions for this format** brings the template back.

## Items

Each row is an item and how it is scored. The buttons on a row move it up or down, split it at the cursor, merge it with the next, delete it, or add a line of text printed above it (a new prompt for the items that follow). A reverse-scoring suggestion shows its reason; accept or reject it. **Remove empty lines at the end** clears the template's spare rows.

## Subscales and scoring

Name each subscale and give its items as numbers, a range, or odd or even. Choose sum or mean for subscales and the total, and whether the total comes from the items or the subscales. The stated score range is checked against the one Prism Survey computes. **How to read the scores** prints in the researcher copy.

## Sources and licence

The source and any adapted-from source, in APA 7; the licence status and a permission note; and where sources print. If Prism Reference Manager is running, **Link and format with Prism Reference Manager** formats the citation there.

## Saving

**Save** (Ctrl+S) saves to the library; a new scale asks where. **Save as** (Ctrl+Shift+S) saves a copy under a new name.
