# Finding a measure in an article

**Find in an article** opens a journal article as a PDF or a picture of a page. Prism Survey finds the measures in it and lists each one with the page and the reasons it was found; choose one to confirm. Scanned pages are read by the text recognition built into Windows, which takes a little while.

The article's page shows beside the form, with each field outlined in its own colour. Click an outline to go to its field. If something was missed or read wrongly, drag across the page to outline a region and read it again.

If no measure was found, choose **Open the article and outline the items myself**.

**Benchmark mode** (a switch on the Scales tab) saves each confirmed article as an answer key for testing how accurately measures are found.
