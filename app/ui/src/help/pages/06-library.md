# Your library

The library is a folder of `.pscale` files, by default **Prism Survey Library** in your Documents folder. **Change folder** picks another.

- **Search** matches titles, tags, subscales, items, sources and instructions. Put a phrase in quotation marks, or name a field: `title:`, `tag:`, `item:`, `subscale:`, `author:`, `year:`.
- **Tag**, **Format** and **Points** narrow the list. With a tag chosen, **Rename or merge this tag** renames it on every scale that has it; a name already in use merges the two.
- A scale that is a variant of another says so under its title.

Click a scale to open it. With a questionnaire open, **Add to questionnaire** beside a scale adds a copy of it.
