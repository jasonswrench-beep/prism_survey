# Change the context

**Change the context** makes a linked variant of a saved scale for a new target: for example, a scale about physicians reworded for nurses.

1. Give the old target as the items name it, the new target, and an optional context. Add any other wording to replace.
2. **Replace the target words** shows each original item beside its new wording, with the changed words marked. Accept each one, edit it, or flag an item that cannot be reworded without changing what it asks (a flagged item keeps the original wording, with your reason).
3. **Make the variant** opens it on the form to save.

A variant keeps its original's structure locked: the items and their order, points and anchors, reverse items, subscales and scoring. Only wording changes. When the original changes later, the variant lists those changes; **Carry into the variant** or **Leave it** for each. The researcher copy says that reliability and validity reported for the original do not carry over.
