# The Questionnaire tab

A questionnaire puts scales and blocks in order and prints them as one Word document. Start with **New questionnaire**, open a saved one, or open the sample.

## Adding

The panel on the left adds:

- Scales from **your library** (search by title or tag) and the **practice scales**.
- **Add the scale on the form**, or **Import a stored scale or questionnaire** (every scale found in the file is added).
- **Blocks**: a short or long [cover letter](topic:cover-letters), a section, a transition sentence, and [demographic questions](topic:demographics).

## Boxes and sections

Each part is a box. Click its name to edit it; a selected scale's details show on the right, and **Open** takes it to the scale form (**Back to the questionnaire** puts your changes in place). Drag a box to move it, or use its arrow buttons, which also work from the keyboard.

A **section** is a named group: the boxes after its heading, up to the next section, belong to it, and moving a section moves them all. Tick **Start this section on a new page** to begin it on a new page. Removing a section's heading keeps its boxes.

The cover letter is always first and prints on its own page.

## How it prints

Item numbers can start again at 1 for each scale or run on; scale titles can be shown, replaced by neutral labels ("Section 3") or hidden on the respondent copy; sources print under each scale, together at the end, or not at all. Section headings always print on the researcher copy, and on the respondent copy unless you turn that off.

## Suggestions and updates

**Suggest measures** offers measures that could sit beside the ones already chosen, each with its reasons. **Bring scales up to date from the library** updates the questionnaire's copies from your saved scales.
