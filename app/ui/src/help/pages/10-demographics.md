# Demographic questions

A demographic block holds questions you type, one per line, with **Add a ready-made question** for 31 questions in four groups: about you, school and work, home and household, and where you live.

A ready-made question goes in as text you can still edit: the question, then one line per answer choice starting with ☐, or a blank (______) for a written answer. In Word, the choice lines print indented under their question and stay on its page.

Every closed question ends with "Prefer not to say", ranges do not overlap, and the race and ethnicity question follows the 2024 federal standard, mark all that apply. Placing sensitive questions near the end of a questionnaire is usual practice.
