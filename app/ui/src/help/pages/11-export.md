# Exporting to Word

A scale or a questionnaire exports to Word in two copies:

- **Respondent copy**: what respondents see.
- **Researcher copy**: the same, plus the scoring key, and the self-check's open findings and source notes.

**Copy for Word** copies the scale for pasting into a Word document that is already open.

When a questionnaire's respondent copy includes a published scale whose licence is unknown or local only, the export reminds you that reproducing it may need the rights holder's permission.
