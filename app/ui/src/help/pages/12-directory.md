# The Comm Directory

The Comm Directory is the *Directory of Communication Related Mental Measures*, searchable inside Prism Survey. **About this directory** shows the book's preface.

Search by words or a phrase in quotation marks (`purpose:` and `author:` name a field), and narrow by chapter. Each entry gives the measure's purpose, items, format, original sample, reliability, validity, critique, source and related research. A reference that matched its published record says so; one that needs a person to look at it says that too.

The directory describes a measure; its items are in the source. Bring it in by:

- **Find it in the article (PDF)**: see [Finding a measure in an article](topic:article).
- **Import a stored copy**: see [Importing a stored scale](topic:import).
- **Type it into a blank scale**, linked to the entry.

**Copy the reference** copies the APA 7 reference.
